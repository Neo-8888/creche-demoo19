Our Learning Programs

Age-appropriate care and education for every stage of early childhood development

Baby Care Program

For infants 6-18 months

Nurturing Our Little Ones

Our baby program creates a safe, warm, and stimulating environment for our youngest learners with individualized care and attention.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Secure attachment and bonding with caregivers
  • Sensory stimulation and exploration activities
  • Early motor skills development
  • Language exposure through songs and stories
  • Individualized feeding and nap schedules

Daily Schedule:

7:00 AM - Arrival & Free Play

9:00 AM - Morning Snack

9:30 AM - Sensory Activities

11:00 AM - Outdoor Time

12:00 PM - Lunch & Nap Time

3:00 PM - Afternoon Snack

3:30 PM - Music & Movement

5:00 PM - Departure

Toddler Program

For children 18 months - 3 years

Exploring the World

Our toddler program encourages curiosity, independence, and social skills through play-based learning in a structured yet flexible environment.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Social skills development and sharing
  • Language and communication skills
  • Fine and gross motor skills refinement
  • Potty training support and guidance
  • Creative expression through art and music

Daily Schedule:

7:00 AM - Arrival & Free Play

8:30 AM - Circle Time (songs, weather)

9:00 AM - Morning Snack

9:30 AM - Theme-Based Learning Activity

10:30 AM - Outdoor Play

12:00 PM - Lunch & Quiet Time

2:00 PM - Art/Creative Activity

3:00 PM - Afternoon Snack

3:30 PM - Story Time

5:00 PM - Departure

Preschool Program

For children 3-5 years (Grade R)

Ready for School

Our preschool program prepares children for formal schooling with a balanced approach to academic, social, and emotional development.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Early literacy and numeracy foundation
  • Problem-solving and critical thinking skills
  • Social-emotional development
  • Independence and responsibility
  • Creative thinking and self-expression

Daily Schedule:

2:00 PM - Creative Arts/Science

3:00 PM - Afternoon Snack

3:30 PM - Story & Review of Day

5:00 PM - Departure
