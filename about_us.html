Welcome to Our Creche Family

In 2010, Mrs. Sibongile Mthembu opened her home and heart to the children of Soshanguve Block FF. What started as a small home-based daycare has grown into a beloved community creche that has cared for hundreds of children over the past 14 years.

Our name "Sibongile" means "We are thankful" in isiZulu. This isn't just a name - it's our philosophy. We're thankful for every child who walks through our doors, every parent who trusts us, and every day we get to do what we love.

Our Promise: To provide a safe, loving environment where children can learn, play, and grow while their parents work or study with peace of mind.
